Privacy Policy

ToolsHub Premium

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

ToolsHub Premium is a static, client-side collection of utilities (calculator, calendar, world clock, converter, timer, notepad, and related tools). This page explains what happens to your data when you use the site.

No Account, No Server-Side Storage

ToolsHub does not require an account, login, or personal information to use. The site runs entirely in your browser and does not operate a backend server that collects or stores your activity.

Local Storage on Your Device

Some tools — such as the Notepad, tool pins, and theme preference — save data using your browser's local storage so your work persists between visits. This data:

  • Stays on your device only
  • Is never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data

Third-Party Services

ToolsHub loads a small number of third-party resources to function, such as icon fonts and utility libraries served from public content delivery networks (CDNs). These providers may process standard technical information (like IP address) as part of delivering those files, under their own privacy policies. ToolsHub does not share any of your personal data with them.

If you use the feedback button, it opens a Google Form in a new tab; any information you choose to submit there is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Cookies and Tracking

ToolsHub does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking/analytics scripts to profile visitors.

Children's Privacy

ToolsHub does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since no personal information is collected by the site itself.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site. Continued use of ToolsHub after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Feedback button on the main site.
